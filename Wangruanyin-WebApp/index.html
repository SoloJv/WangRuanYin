🌐 Website mode apply 王软音 to any website, automatically

Type a website and click Open with 王软音 — it opens in a new tab of this web app, the page is fetched automatically through Jina Reader (a service that re-hosts any page server-side and returns its real HTML), Wikipedia's own CORS API for articles, plus public CORS proxies, and rendered there with pinyin, translations & HSK colours applied automatically (a floating panel lets you adjust the toggles on the page, just like the browser extension). Your toggles and HSK choices from above are used automatically. If fetching the raw page fails, the app falls back to a clean readable render of the same content.

Advanced — bookmarklet for sites the reader can't fetch (login walls, heavy JavaScript)

Some websites can't be fetched into the reader (login-required, IP-blocked to proxies, or only client-side rendered). For those, drag the 王软音 link to your bookmarks bar, open the real site, and click the bookmark — the floating panel appears on the page itself.
